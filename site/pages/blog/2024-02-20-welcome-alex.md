We are adding a third voice to this blog. Alex Example has been working with Twig and
static-site workflows for years and will write about templates, caching and the small
details that make a site pleasant to maintain.

## What Alex will cover

Expect posts about template structure, reusable includes and the generated directories
that Herbie manages for you. The aim is practical guidance you can apply to this
starter without installing anything extra.

## A growing example

More authors also means more bylines to demonstrate. Each post links to an author
profile, and each profile lists the posts written by that person. Add your own profile
by copying a file in `site/pages/blog/author` and using a new `author_id`.
